# Vision

## Mission

Aidos is an AI Operating Environment—a personal, offline-first platform where AI agents become productivity tools under your direct control.

## Punchline

*Tweak underground, on a train.*

## Core Principles

**Offline-first**: Works without internet. Sync when connected.

**Open source**: Fully transparent. Community-driven.

**AI Operating Environment**: Native integration of AI reasoning into every layer—not a chatbot wrapper.

**Coding first**: Automation through code, not button clicks.

**Future personal productivity**: A platform for how we'll work when AI is everywhere.
